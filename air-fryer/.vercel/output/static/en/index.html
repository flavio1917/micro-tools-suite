Conversion Guide: Oven vs Air Fryer

Found a perfect oven recipe but want to cook it in your air fryer to save time and energy? Our air fryer calculator applies the standard algorithm: reducing the temperature by about 25°F (20°C) and cooking time by 20%.

Quick Temp Table (Conventional Oven → Air Fryer)

Conventional OvenAir FryerNotes
350°F (180°C) 320°F (160°C) Ideal for cakes and baking
400°F (200°C) 350°F (180°C) Perfect for chicken and fries
430°F (220°C) 400°F (200°C) For crispy gratins

⚡ Fan or Conventional Oven?

An air fryer works like a compact super-convection oven. If your recipe is for a conventional oven, drop the temp by 25°F. If it's already for a fan/convection oven, you often just reduce the time, but a slight temp drop prevents drying out the food.

🍟 Fries and Frozen Foods

For frozen foods (fries, nuggets), the rule shifts: you can often keep the same temperature listed for a fan oven, but reduce the time by 20-30%. Remember to shake the basket halfway!

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to preheat my air fryer?

Unlike traditional ovens, most modern air fryers reach target temps in 2-3 minutes. Preheating is only recommended for searing meats (like steaks) or baking, to ensure an immediate thermal shock.

Can I use parchment paper or foil?

Yes, but carefully. Parchment paper must be perforated for air circulation. NEVER put parchment paper in during preheating without food on top: the airflow will blow it into the heating element and burn it.
