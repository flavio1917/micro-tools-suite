Breaded Fish Sticks

A preparation of homemade breaded white fish sticks.

Temperature
350 °F
Cook Time
8-10 min
Prep Time
15 minutes
Servings
4
pers.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 14 oz thick white fish fillet (e.g., cod, plaice, hake)
  • 1.4 oz all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp fish spice mix (e.g., dried parsley, lemon zest)
  • 1 to 2 beaten eggs
  • 4.2 oz fine breadcrumbs or Panko
  • Cooking spray
  • Salt and pepper

👨‍🍳 Instructions

1

Ensure fish is boneless. Pat thoroughly dry with paper towels. Cut into rectangular sticks approximately 1 inch thick.

2

In a shallow dish, combine flour, spice mix, salt, and pepper. In a second dish, beat the eggs. In a third dish, place the breadcrumbs.

3

Dredge each fish stick in the flour mixture, shaking off excess. Dip in the beaten egg, then coat completely with breadcrumbs, pressing to adhere.

4

Arrange the sticks in a single layer in the cooking basket. Spray the breading evenly with cooking spray.

5

Cook at 350°F for 8-10 minutes. Halfway through cooking, gently turn the sticks with a spatula to ensure even browning.

💡

Chef's Tip

"The fish sticks are delicate. To prevent breakage, use a silicone spatula instead of tongs when turning them."
